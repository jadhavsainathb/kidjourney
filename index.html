01Overview

KidJourney ("the App," "we," "us") is a mobile application that helps parents and guardians organize their children's daily tasks, calendars, timetables, achievements, and routines in one place. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

The App is developed and operated by Sainath Jadhav (contact details below). By creating an account and using KidJourney, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect the email address and password you provide. Your password is never stored in readable form — it is handled entirely by our authentication provider using industry-standard hashing.

Child profile information. As the account holder, you may enter information about your child or children, which can include their name, date of birth, school, grade, and (if you choose to add one later) a profile photo.

Activity and organizational data. Information you or your child (via Kid Mode, under your account) create while using the App, including tasks, calendar events, timetable entries, goals, and reward point settings.

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, microphone or camera data, or browsing history from other apps or websites.

03How we use information

  • To operate the core features of the App — displaying tasks, calendars, timetables, and progress for the children on your account.
  • To let you sign in securely and keep your family's data private to your account.
  • To calculate reward points and goal progress that you've configured.
  • To schedule the reminders you set up (see Notifications below).
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

We do not use your information, or your child's information, for advertising, and we do not build behavioral profiles for marketing purposes.

04Data storage & security

App data is stored using Google's Firebase platform (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Data is encrypted in transit between your device and our servers.

Access to your data is restricted by security rules so that only your own signed-in account can read or write information belonging to your family — no other user of the App can see your children's information, tasks, or schedules.

05Third-party services

We rely on the following infrastructure providers to operate the App:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — manages sign-in and account security.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google) — stores your account's data.

We do not integrate any advertising networks, third-party analytics SDKs, or social media tracking tools. No data collected by the App is sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

06Notifications

Reminders for tasks, events, and schedules are scheduled and delivered entirely on your own device using its built-in notification system. This information is never transmitted to, or processed by, any server — it stays on your device.

07Children's privacy & Kid Mode

KidJourney is designed to be used by parents and guardians to manage information about their children — it is not designed for children to sign up or provide information on their own. The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children; every piece of information about a child is entered and controlled by the parent or guardian who owns the account.

Kid Mode is an optional, simplified view of the parent's own account, protected by a PIN that only the parent sets. It is not a separate account, does not collect any additional information, and cannot be used to create or modify a child's profile.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information outside of the account you control, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

08Data retention & deletion

We retain your account's data for as long as your account remains active. You can delete individual tasks, events, timetable entries, and goals directly within the App at any time.

To delete a child's full profile or your entire account and all associated data, please contact us at the email address below. We will process deletion requests within 10 business days.

09Your choices & rights

  • You can review and edit the information you've entered about your children at any time within the App.
  • You can turn off notifications at any time through your device's system settings.
  • You can request a copy of the data associated with your account, or request that it be deleted, by contacting us.

10Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy — such as a change to what information we collect or how it's used — we will update the date at the top of this page and, where required, notify you within the App before the changes take effect.
