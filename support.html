How do I reset my password?

On the Log In screen, tap Forgot Password? and enter your email. If an account exists for that address, a reset link will be sent to it.

How do I delete a child's profile?

Open the child's details, tap the edit (pencil) icon, then tap the delete icon in the top right. This permanently removes that child and everything linked to them — tasks, events, timetable entries, and goals.

How do I delete my entire account?

From the Home screen, tap the gear icon to open Account Settings, then Delete Account under the Danger Zone. You'll be asked to confirm your password before it's removed. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is Kid Mode?

Kid Mode is a simplified, PIN-protected view of your account that you can hand to your child — showing their tasks and schedule for the day, without access to any settings or other children's information.
